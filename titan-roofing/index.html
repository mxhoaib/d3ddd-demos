About

Titan Roofing and Gutter Cleaning

Titan Roofing is a local, family-owned roofing contractor specialising in residential roofing, gutter cleaning and snow removal on the North Shore of Chicago and the Chicago Northwest suburbs. Licensed, bonded and insured.

  • Licensed, bonded and insured
  • Local and family-owned
  • Materials: GAF, Tamko, Elk, Owens Corning, IKO and CertainTeed shingles
  • Guarantee stated on their site: asphalt shingle roofs 10 years, flat roofs 7 years
