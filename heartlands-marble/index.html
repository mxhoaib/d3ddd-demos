About

Heartlands Marble

Heartlands Marble have been manufacturing fireplaces since 1995. A small, family-run Midlands business and proud British manufacturer, their skilled masons make made-to-measure marble fireplaces, marble sets, granite, slate and limestone using both traditional methods and state-of-the-art CNC machinery, with quality European micro marble from Italy and Spain.

  • Manufacturing fireplaces since 1995
  • Family-run, proud British manufacturer
  • Traditional masonry plus CNC machinery
  • European micro marble from Italy and Spain
