About

Solid Edge Wood Products

Solid Edge Wood Products makes custom handcrafted cabinetry and furniture from locally sourced Ontario hardwoods. Not a high-volume shop, they focus on each individual piece, aiming to create sustainable custom furniture and cabinetry that lasts for generations.

  • Family owned and operated
  • Traditional craftsmanship
  • Sustainable materials and practices
  • Crafted in Ontario, Canada
