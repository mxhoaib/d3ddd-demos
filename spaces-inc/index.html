About

Spaces Inc.

Spaces Inc. specializes in the design, build and installation of custom cabinetry for residential homes and commercial spaces, serving customers across Ontario for over 20 years. They design, manufacture and install custom kitchens, bathroom vanities, walk-in closets, mudrooms, offices, home theatres and built-ins.

  • Over 20 years in business
  • Residential and commercial
  • Process: appointment, preparation, installation
  • Serving customers across Ontario
