About

Samwood Kitchens

Samwood Kitchens offers a wide range of custom-made cabinetry in the GTA, Ontario. They provide design assistance and advice with 3D drawings, factory-direct custom cabinets in solid wood and a wide variety of finishes, and experienced installers who pay close attention to detail.
