About

HLuu’s Concreting

HLuu’s Concreting was established in 2020, with over 20 years’ experience in concrete works and structural landscaping. Specialising in new builds and knock-down rebuilds, they help from site-cut retaining walls to driveways, concrete surrounds and final landscaping touches, servicing all areas of Brisbane and the Gold Coast.

  • Established 2020
  • Over 20 years’ experience
  • New builds and knock-down rebuilds
  • QBCC licensed
