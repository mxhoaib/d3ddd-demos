About

GCC Concepts Ltd

GCC Concepts Ltd provides all-inclusive shopfitting and retail interior fit-outs, from design and project management through manufacturing and installation. A team of specialist technicians and skilled craftsmen, they work with retail outlets, restaurants, takeaways and estate agents, and make specialist counters for bars, restaurants, receptions and retail.

  • Design, manufacture and installation
  • Project management throughout
  • Certified gas and electrical team
  • Workshop-cut solid surfaces
